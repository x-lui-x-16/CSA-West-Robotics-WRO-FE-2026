# 🔧 Ackermann Steering Mechanism
The front wheels use an Ackermann-style steering linkage, actuated by the MG996R servo. This geometry allows the inner and outer wheels to follow different turning radii during a turn, just like in real cars, reducing tire slip and improving traction and stability.

The linkage was custom-designed and 3D-printed to fit the robot's chassis and wheel spacing.
