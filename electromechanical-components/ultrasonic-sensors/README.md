# 📏 Ultrasonic Sensors (HC-SR04)
Three HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors are mounted on the front of the robot: left, center, and right. Each one measures distance in a range of roughly 2–400 cm with about 3 mm accuracy.

The center sensor detects when the robot is approaching the front wall, triggering a turn, while the left and right sensors are compared to decide the turn direction.
